This page shows your current Classification levels and points.

The scores you are seeing come from your Initial Classification — the one you got at Intake.

Your first Reclassification can happen after 3 months. Reclassification uses a different scoresheet, so your points will likely change even if you did nothing wrong. Many people see their points go up. This is normal, and it is not a punishment.

Be careful: disciplinary infractions can raise your points and custody level fast, and it can take years to bring them back down. A low score now is not something you keep automatically.

If it has been more than 3 months and you have not been reclassified, ask your COIII.
