ION is a website that helps you get ready for release. You can use it on the computers in your Unit's Library. It works a little like a dating site, but for reentry — you make a profile, search for Service Provider organizations on the outside that can help you, and send them messages. You can start using ION up to 11 months before your release date, and you keep the same account after you go home.

See your Unit Librarian or Peer Support Specialist to get started. If you can't get to the Unit Library, ask your COIII to help you sign up.

## How can ION help me?

ION helps you connect with Service Provider organizations. A service provider is an organization on the outside that works with people coming home from prison. ION has nine kinds:

- Shelters & Housing
- Support Networks (peer, family, and more)
- Multiservice Agencies (counseling, food, clothing, and more)
- Churches and Ministries
- Substance Abuse Treatment & Recovery
- Health Care (including behavioral health and dental)
- Legal Matters
- Employment & Job Training
- Education

## How ION works

You search near the address where you think you will be released. Providers closest to that address show up first.

![A screenshot of the ION Service Provider Search page showing a list of provider categories to search.](image-placeholder-1)

You can open any provider to learn more about them, and save the ones you like so they are easy to find next time.

![A screenshot of the ION Multiservice Agencies search results page, showing a list of providers and a map of their locations.](image-placeholder-2)

Providers can find you, too. Once you make your profile, providers near your release address can see it, and they may message you first.

## Questions

### How do I sign up?

Ask your Peer Support Specialist, Unit Librarian, or COIII for an ION Returning Citizen Registration Worksheet. The worksheet asks for your name, your ADC number, the username and password you want to use, the address where you expect to be released, and the kinds of help you are looking for — this is the information used to set up your account and find providers near you.

Fill it out, then bring the finished worksheet with you when you go to the Unit Library to make your account. You will use the username and password you chose to log in.

### When can I use ION?

Talk with your Peer Support Specialist, Unit Librarian, or COIII to set up a time. Every unit does this a little differently, so they can tell you how it works where you are.

### What if I can't get to the Unit Library?

Ask your COIII to help you sign up. You can also choose to share your username and password with someone you trust on the outside. They can log in for you, look for providers, and message them. Talk with them often about what they find.

### How does messaging work?

You can message any provider you find. Messages are meant for two things: figuring out whether a provider is a good fit for you, and planning your reentry with them. ADCRR staff can read the messages you send and receive while you are in prison, and once you send a message you cannot change it or take it back. After you are released, your messages are not visible to the Department of Corrections.

### What if I forget my username or password?

See your Peer Support Specialist or Unit Librarian for help.

### What happens after I am released?

Your account stays yours. You can log in from any computer or smartphone. The providers you saved and the messages you sent will still be there.

### Do I have to sign up?

No. ION is your choice and is not required by ADCRR. It is a tool to help you work on your own reentry, and it is free for you, for returning citizens, and for your loved ones.
