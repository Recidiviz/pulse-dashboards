- **Going longer without a disciplinary infraction:** This is the biggest one. Your score changes based on how many tickets you got in the last 3 years and how serious your worst ticket was. Disciplinary infractions count for fewer points as time passes. The disciplinaries you got in the last 3 years count the most. Your most serious ticket keeps affecting your score for longer than the rest.
- **Finishing a Major Program:** You get −5 points for the first Major Program you finish and −3 for the second. That is −8 total, and it is the most you can get from programs during this incarceration. If you have already finished two Major Programs, even if they were a long time ago, more programs will not lower your points further.
- **Getting older:** Points go down a little as you move into older age groups. This does not happen every year.
- **Older charges and older escape history:** Things from further in the past count for less over time.

Major Programs and time are the only things that lower your classification points. Other work — like being a Peer Support Specialist or teaching a class — does not lower your points, even though it can help you in other ways.
